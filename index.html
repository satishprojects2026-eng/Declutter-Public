Meeting notes, decluttered.

Record a meeting, get clean notes and action items in seconds. Audio never leaves your phone.

On-device transcription

Apple's Speech framework converts your audio to text directly on your iPhone. The audio file is never uploaded.

AI cleanup

The transcript is sent to a private backend that calls Anthropic Claude to produce a title, summary, key topics, decisions, and action items.

Share anywhere

Export notes as PDF or Markdown, or send a clean HTML email straight from the app.

Your data, your control

Delete any meeting at any time. Delete your account and everything goes with it within 30 days.

Declutter is currently in development for iOS. Sign-in supports Apple, Google, Yahoo, email + password, and magic link.
